### Fixed

- Scattered wrong glyphs across several panes at once during CJK-heavy sessions. When the shared WebGL glyph atlas's page pool fills (16 pages on Apple Silicon; Hangul's 11,172 distinct syllables fill it fast), its page-merge path invalidates sibling panes' cached glyph geometry; every pane now listens for that merge and rebuilds its glyph model immediately, so the corruption can no longer stick until a new pane happens to be opened. (#741)
